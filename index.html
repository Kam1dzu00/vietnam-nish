01 · The concept

A modern perfume house drawn from Vietnam’s nature, its architecture and its ornament.

The brief was clear: minimalism that is never boring, with one artistic signature and no souvenir clichés. The same visual language has to work on social media, in the boutique, on bags and on the team’s uniforms.

Our signature is the lacquer-red line: a fine ink drawing filled with cinnabar and lit with cream, set on rice paper, velvet or gold. Since the direction is still open, we present all of it, organised into three colour worlds.

01

Nature

Lotus, crane, apricot blossom and bamboo are living symbols, drawn with one fine ink line.

02

Architecture

Pagoda roofs, lattice screens and seal frames give the system its geometry.

03

Landscape

Ha Long’s karst peaks and the red sun bring horizon, air and calm.

The signature · six marks

Signing Vietnam Nish

02 · Quá trình

How we got here

01 / 08

01

The brief

Bản tóm tắt

You asked for a premium, modern brand built from Vietnam’s nature, architecture and ornament, with no souvenir clichés. Minimalism, but never boring.

02

First sketches

Phác thảo đầu tiên

We started with loose motif sheets: lotus, crane, karst bay, pagoda roof and red sun, to find one drawing hand for the whole brand.

03

Building the motifs

Họa tiết

The motifs became one family, drawn in lacquer red, ink and cream, then framed as square seals and tiles.

04

First patterns

Hoa văn đầu tiên

We cut the strongest pieces out of the boards and tested them as repeats on light and dark grounds.

05

Studies for the mark

Biểu tượng

Lotus, roof and flower studies led to the emblems, the seal and the script you will see below.

06

A cooler palette

Bảng màu lam

We also tried indigo and gold, a blue-and-white porcelain mood, to see how far the motifs can travel.

07

Craft and finish

Chất liệu

Blind emboss, gold leaf and fine line work: the tactile layer that turns a pattern into a luxury object.

08

Systems

Hệ thống

Finally we turned it into systems: modular seal tiles and lattice connectors that work at any size.
